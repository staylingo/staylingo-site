Questions

Before you ask

What counts as one listing?

One bookable property in your channel manager. An apartment is one listing; a villa is one listing. Simple.

Do I need Beds24 or Smoobu?

Yes — StayLingo plugs into your channel manager, which is how it reads your calendar and reaches Airbnb and Booking.com messages. Beds24 first, Smoobu right behind it.

What does “fixed for life” mean?

Founding hosts pay €9 per listing per month, for as long as they stay subscribed — and the rate itself never goes up — even when public prices rise. It's our thank-you for trusting us early.

Can I pay annually?

Yes — use the Annual switch above: every plan is 12 months for the price of 10 (Founding €90, Starter €140, Pro €240 per listing per year), billed once a year. The founding rate stays fixed on annual billing too.

When do I pay?

After your free week, only if you stay. No card is taken to start the free week.

Can I cancel?

Any time, in two clicks. No lock-in, no exit call, no guilt trip.
