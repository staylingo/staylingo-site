FAQ

Questions hosts ask us

Does it work with Booking.com too?

Yes. StayLingo connects through your channel manager (Beds24 today, Smoobu next), so it answers guests from Airbnb, Booking.com and your other connected channels in one place — each guest in their own language.

What languages does it speak?

30+ languages, including German, French, Dutch, Italian, Spanish and Portuguese — the languages your European guests actually write in. StayLingo detects each guest's language automatically and shows you the full conversation translated into English.

Can I review messages before they're sent?

Yes. Start in draft mode: StayLingo prepares every reply and you approve it with one tap. Switch any listing to auto-send whenever you're ready — you can switch back at any time.

How can it promise an early check-in?

It doesn't guess. Before answering, StayLingo checks your real calendar and cleaning schedule, so an early check-in or late checkout is only offered when the property is actually free and cleanable in time. If it isn't, the guest gets an honest alternative instead.

What does StayLingo never answer on its own?

Complaints, refund requests and emergencies are never auto-answered. Those are escalated to you immediately with a WhatsApp alert, with the conversation translated into English so you can act fast.

Is my guests' data safe?

Yes — StayLingo is hosted in the EU and built on GDPR basics: guest data is used only to answer that guest, never sold or used for advertising, and you can request deletion of your data at any time.
