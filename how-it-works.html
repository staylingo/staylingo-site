How it works · Guidance

Live in an afternoon. Silent ever after.

No new app for your guests, no new habits for you. StayLingo sits inside the tools you already use — here's the whole journey, honestly, step by step.

1

Create your account

Sign up with Google, email or phone on the sign-up page. Founding hosts are activated personally — we onboard the first 20 one by one, so your setup is done with you, not dumped on you.

2

Connect Beds24 or Smoobu

One secure connection to your channel manager. That's how StayLingo reads your real calendar and reaches the guest messages from Airbnb and Booking.com — no passwords shared, disconnect any time.

3

Teach it your property (10 minutes)

Check-in instructions, door codes, Wi-Fi, parking, house rules, your upsell prices and your tone — warm, formal, playful. StayLingo answers as you, in 30+ languages.

4

Guests write. It answers.

Every message answered in under a minute, in the guest's language, calendar-checked. Upsells offered with payment links when the moment is right.

5

You watch — in English

Your inbox shows every conversation translated. Jump in and take over any chat with one tap; StayLingo politely steps aside.

6

Serious things find you instantly

Complaints, refunds, emergencies: never auto-answered. You get a WhatsApp alert with a suggested reply, and you decide. That's the rule — it never changes.

Host guidance

Get the most from StayLingo

📝 Write your house guide once

The better your guide (parking spot, quiet hours, best bakery nearby), the better every answer in every language. Ten minutes here saves you hundreds of replies.

💶 Price your upsells honestly

Late checkout at €20–€30 converts best across Europe. StayLingo offers it only when the next guest's arrival makes it safe.

👀 Glance at the inbox weekly

Not to work — to spot patterns. Three guests asking about the same thing means your listing page needs one new sentence. We'll point it out.

🛡️ Keep the safety rule sacred

If a message ever feels wrong, take over. Human judgment always outranks the AI — in the product, not just in this text.
