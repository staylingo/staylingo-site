Services

A full front desk, working every hour you don't.

Six services, one subscription. Every one of them exists to answer a single question hosts ask us: “who replies to my guests when I'm not there?”

🌍

1 · Multilingual AI replies

Every guest message answered in the guest's own language — in under a minute, 24/7. Trained on real host conversations: directions, Wi-Fi, parking, check-in codes, local tips.

  • 30+ languages, native-level tone
  • Your house rules and your voice, not a robot's
  • You read everything in English
Included in Starter & Pro
📅

2 · Calendar-checked answers

Connected to your Beds24 or Smoobu calendar, StayLingo checks real availability before answering availability questions — so it never promises a 1 AM check-in you can't honour.

  • Early check-in / late arrival checks
  • Extra-night offers when the calendar is free
  • Airbnb + Booking.com through your channel manager
Included in Starter & Pro
💶

3 · Automatic upsells

Late checkout, early check-in, extra cleaning, airport transfer — offered at the moment the guest is most likely to say yes, with a payment link right in the chat.

  • You set the prices; StayLingo does the selling
  • Payment confirmed before anything is promised
Pro · and for founding hosts
🛡️

4 · Human safety net

Some messages should never be automated. Complaints, refund requests, damage reports and emergencies are never auto-answered — they go straight to you on WhatsApp with a suggested reply.

  • Instant WhatsApp alert to the host
  • Draft reply written for you to approve
Included in Starter & Pro
📥

5 · One translated inbox

Every conversation from every channel in a single view — original language and English side by side, full history, nothing lost between apps.

  • Searchable conversation history
  • See exactly what the AI said, any time
  • Take over a conversation with one tap
Included in Starter & Pro
🤝

6 · Founding-host onboarding

We set up your first listing with you: house rules, check-in instructions, upsell prices, tone of voice. Ten minutes on a call, then it runs itself.

  • Personal setup for the first 20 hosts
  • Honest feedback loop — you shape the product
Founding hosts only

See it work on your own listing — free.
